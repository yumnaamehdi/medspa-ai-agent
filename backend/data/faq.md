# Frequently Asked Questions

## How long does lip filler last?
Lip filler duration varies by product, treatment area, metabolism, and other individual factors. A licensed provider should discuss expected longevity during consultation.

## Is there downtime after lip filler?
Temporary swelling, bruising, tenderness, and redness may occur after treatment.

## Can I get treated the same day as my consultation?
Same-day treatment may be possible depending on provider availability, medical assessment, and treatment eligibility.

## Do you require a deposit?
Yes. A $100 deposit is required for in-person appointments and is applied toward treatment.

## What is the cancellation policy?
At least 24 hours notice is required for cancellation.

## Can the AI tell me which treatment I medically need?
No. The AI can explain services and general information but should not diagnose conditions or determine medical eligibility.
